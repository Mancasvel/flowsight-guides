A small trial with a clear purpose

Choose one question tied to a real task. Compare summaries with actual outcomes and add contributions outside computer context. Keep observations separate from explanations, try one small adjustment and set a review date. The goal is a decision that helps the work you are doing.

FlowSight analyzes raw screen context locally. Optional cloud, integration and sharing capabilities require a separate review of their information journey. A summary is an interpretation; it does not establish outcome quality, capture every contribution or diagnose a person's health.

These are associated FlowSight resources with common ownership. The examples and downloadable worksheets are writing and review aids, usable in ordinary tools with or without the app. They do not imply automatic integration or measured customer improvements.
